    <div class="rh-generic--component">
        <p>With the impending end of life for CentOS Linux<sup>®</sup> 7 in June 2024, the clock is ticking for organizations relying on it.</p>

<p>But there's a silver lining - this presents an opportunity to redefine your IT strategy.</p>

<p>Join our webinar on Nov 30, "Ask Red Hat Experts Anything on CentOS Linux End Of Life &amp; Migration to Red Hat Enterprise Linux," and get clarity on:</p>

<ul style="margin-top: 3px;">
<li>The state of CentOS Linux and its discontinuation</li>
<li>Key benefits of switching to Red Hat Enterprise Linux</li>
<li>A seamless transition plan for migrating to Red Hat Enterprise Linux</li>
</ul>

<p>Prepare for the upcoming CentOS Linux end of life, and explore what's possible with RHEL.</p>

<p><b>WHO SHOULD ATTEND?</b><br />
This webinar is ideal for IT decision makers, system administrators, and infrastructure architects with a technical background who have a role to play in migrating from CentOS to a new operating system.</p>

<p><b>Workshop details</b><br />
Date : Nov 30, 2023<br />
Time : 30-45 minutes<br />
Location : Virtual via On24</p>

<p>If you have any questions prior to our webinar, please reach out<a href="mailto:aapramey@redhat.com" style="color: #cc0000; text-decoration: none;"> aapramey@redhat.com</a>.</p>
